Privacy Policy for Money Saver

Effective date: October 8, 2026
App: Money Saver (Android, package com.black.kohi.moneysaver)
Developer: Black-Kohi
Contact: black.kohi.devi@gmail.com

Summary: Money Saver is a personal finance tracker that works on your device. I do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal or financial data. The app has no user accounts, no developer server, no advertising, and no analytics.

1. Data you enter in the app

You may enter accounts and balances, transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals, debts, recurring bills, custom asset and currency-rate settings, a profile name, bio and picture, and app settings.

This data is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. I cannot see it, access it, or recover it.

2. Data sent over the internet

To keep currency and crypto values up to date, the app sends requests to:

  • open.er-api.com: fiat exchange rates.
  • CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com): crypto and stablecoin prices. The request includes the IDs of the coins being priced, which can include custom coin IDs you add.
  • Any web link you add for a custom asset or currency rate: the app requests that link directly.

These services can see technical information that comes with any internet request, such as your IP address. They handle it under their own privacy policies. Your balances, transactions, and personal details are never sent in these requests.

3. Backups

  • The app does not use Android system backup, cloud backup, or automatic device-to-device transfer of its data.
  • You can export a backup file from the app and save it wherever you choose. It contains your financial data, so you are responsible for keeping it safe.
  • You are responsible for your own backups. If you lose your device, uninstall the app, or clear its data without an exported backup, your data cannot be recovered.

4. App lock and biometrics

If you turn on app lock, authentication is handled by the Android system. The app does not receive, see, or store your fingerprint or face data.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: used only to fetch exchange rates and prices as described above.

6. Third-party services

The app includes no advertising or analytics services. The only third parties involved are the rate and price providers listed in section 2, plus any link you choose to add.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Your rights

Because your data stays on your device and I hold none of it, you control it fully: view, edit, export, or delete it inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. If you have questions about privacy rights where you live (for example under the GDPR, the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, or the CCPA), contact me at the email above.

9. Data retention

I retain no user data. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

10. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please use a screen lock and keep any exported backups somewhere safe.

11. Financial disclaimer

Money Saver is a tracking tool. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and it does not hold, move, or trade money. Exchange rates and prices come from third parties and may be delayed or inaccurate.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update the effective date at the top and publish the new version at this same page.
